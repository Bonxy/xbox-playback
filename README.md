# 🎮 Xbox Playback: Bonxy

A one-page infographic of my Xbox history from 2008 to 2026, built from my data in Xbox's 25th anniversary **Playback** archive.

**Live page:** [xbox-playback.bonxy.org](https://xbox-playback.bonxy.org)

## What's on it

- **Headline stats:** 7,298 hours played, 90,760 Gamerscore, 240 games played and 74 completed
- **Origin:** first console (Xbox 360), first game, first achievement and first 100% completion
- **#1 most played game:** Grand Theft Auto V, with 542 hours
- **Top genres and platforms:** mostly racing, almost all on console
- **Peak years:** 2012 and 2013
- **Game records:** the Forza Horizon series holds all three
- **Legacy:** rarest achievement, games completed and longest play streak (39 days)
- **All-time top 10 games**

Click any game's box art to open that game's page on my [Xbox Timer](https://projects.bonxy.org/xboxtimer).

## Files
